Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

Click2Visa.asia („we”, „us”, „our”) is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and visa processing services (collectively, the „Service”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to process your Vietnam e‑Visa application and to complete your payment. This includes:

  • Passport details: full name, date of birth, passport number, nationality, issue/expiry dates.
  • Contact information: email address and Telegram ID (to deliver the visa).
  • Travel details: intended date of arrival, port of entry, flight number (if available).
  • Photo: a recent passport‑style photo and a selfie (used only for the visa application).
  • Payment information: processed via NOWPayments (cryptocurrency, USDT TRC-20). We do NOT store your wallet address or private keys.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely for:

  • Submitting your e‑Visa application to the Vietnamese immigration system.
  • Communicating with you regarding your application status (via Telegram or email).
  • Processing payments and issuing refunds (if applicable).
  • Improving our automated AI service (anonymized, aggregated data only).

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Automatic deletion: Once your e‑Visa PDF is successfully delivered to you, all personal data (passport scan, selfie, application form) is automatically and permanently deleted from our active systems immediately. No backup copies are retained. We do not keep any personal data after the visa is sent.

Financial transaction records are kept for 3 years as required by law (NOWPayments handles most of this; we only store transaction IDs and order amounts).

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We share your data only when necessary:

  • Vietnam Immigration Department: the official government portal receives your passport details and photo to issue the visa.
  • NOWPayments (payment processor): for crypto payments – NOWPayments’ privacy policy applies.
  • Telegram / WhatsApp: to provide support – messages are encrypted in transit.
  • Visa processing partner: a trusted third‑party agent who submits the application on our behalf.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or third‑party marketing companies.

5. Security Measures

We use industry‑standard encryption (TLS 1.3) for all data transmission. Access to our servers is restricted and logged. Our AI bot does not store chat history longer than necessary for the current session.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction, or deletion of your data. Contact us at privacy@click2visa.asia. Since we delete data automatically after delivery, most requests will be fulfilled immediately if the application is still pending.

7. International Transfers

Your data is processed in Vietnam (where the visa is issued) and may be stored on servers located in the EU or Singapore, depending on our cloud provider. All transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us to remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The latest version will always be posted at this URL. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact Us

For privacy‑related inquiries, please reach out via Telegram: @Click2VisaBotSupport or email privacy@click2visa.asia.
